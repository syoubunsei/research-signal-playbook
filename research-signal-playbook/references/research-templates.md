# Research Templates

Use these templates as a starting point. Trim sections that do not add value.

## 1. Fast Category Scan

### Research Question
- What category is being studied?
- What decision should this support?

### Market Snapshot
- Why this category matters now
- What has changed recently

### Player Map
| Product | Positioning | Target User | Momentum | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Signal Summary
- Product signals
- Distribution signals
- Social signals
- User signals

### Key Patterns
- Pattern 1
- Pattern 2
- Pattern 3

### Implications
- What the market structure suggests
- Where the opportunity window is

### Recommended Moves
- `P0`
- `P1`
- `P2`

## 2. Competitor Deep Dive

### Research Question
- Why this competitor matters

### Basic Facts
- Product
- Company
- Platforms
- Pricing
- Latest version or recent updates

### Experience Flow
- Entry point
- First key moment
- Core loop
- Shareable moment
- Potential payment trigger

### What Makes It Spread
- Main content angle
- Main social hook
- Main screenshot or story hook

### Product Depth vs Buzz
- Surface attraction
- Deeper retention logic
- Where it may be fragile

### Risks
- Reliability
- Compliance
- Trust
- Team or entity health

### What to Learn
- Borrow
- Avoid
- Watch

## 3. Opportunity Assessment

### Problem Framing
- What user need seems underserved

### Evidence
- User complaints
- Product gaps
- Weaknesses in current leaders
- Channel shifts

### Opportunity Hypothesis
- What wedge exists
- Why now

### Constraints
- Technical difficulty
- Go-to-market difficulty
- Regulatory exposure

### Decision
- Pursue now
- Monitor
- Avoid

1. What is true now
