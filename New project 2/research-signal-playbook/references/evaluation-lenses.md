# Evaluation Lenses

Use these lenses to avoid shallow research.

## 1. Attention Lens

Ask:

- What does a user notice in the first 10 seconds?
- What is the simplest explanation for why someone clicks?
- Is the attraction visual, emotional, functional, or social?

## 2. Spread Lens

Ask:

- What gets screenshotted, reposted, or retold?
- Does the product contain built-in shareable moments?
- Is spread driven by creator education, emotional resonance, or novelty?

## 3. Retention Lens

Ask:

- Why would a user return tomorrow?
- Is there a relationship loop, collection loop, progress loop, or habit cue?
- Does the product deepen over time or only feel fresh once?

## 4. Payment Lens

Ask:

- What moment creates willingness to pay?
- Is payment for access, status, intimacy, convenience, control, or personalization?
- Is monetization aligned with emotional value or does it interrupt it?

## 5. Trust Lens

Ask:

- What could make users hesitate?
- Are there sensitive permissions, legal issues, service instability, or opaque policies?
- What trust-building signals are present or missing?

## 6. Operator Lens

Ask:

- Does the company look capable of sustaining the product?
- Are updates frequent and coherent?
- Are there signs of strong execution, fragmentation, or fragility?

## 7. User Truth Lens

Ask:

- What are users explicitly saying?
- What are they repeatedly asking for?
- What do their behaviors imply even if they do not say it directly?

## 8. Market Structure Lens

Ask:

- Is the category early, crowded, fragmented, or consolidating?
- Is buzz larger than actual adoption?
- Is the moat product depth, content distribution, capital, community, or regulation?
